# Prospect discovery

Use this guide when the customer has no PETAOS tenant, is comparing options, or wants to understand which PETAOS capabilities fit their business before signing in.

## Evidence boundary

- Use current public information from [PETAOS](https://petaos.tech/), [Products](https://petaos.tech/products), [Solutions](https://petaos.tech/solutions), [Resources](https://petaos.tech/resources), and [Book a Demo](https://petaos.tech/demo).
- Do not send an unregistered customer into Console pages or tenant operation manuals as if they already have access.
- Separate public website claims from recommendations. Treat detailed app availability, integrations, pricing, implementation scope, and delivery dates as items to confirm in a demo or proposal.
- Never invent ROI, customer counts, certifications, supported connectors, or translated UI labels. Quote live pricing only after reading the current public pricing page.

## Discover the scenario

Use information the customer already provided. Ask only the missing questions needed to recommend a starting point, normally no more than four:

1. What does the business sell or operate, and which team owns the process?
2. What is the current workflow from trigger to completion?
3. Where is the biggest delay, duplicate entry, missing visibility, or control risk?
4. Which systems, files, messages, payment channels, or warehouses must stay connected?

Restate the scenario before recommending anything. If the need is broad, choose one frequent, measurable workflow as the first pilot instead of presenting all modules.

## Public journey routing

| Customer scenario | Recommended journey | Starting modules | Efficiency opportunity | Good first demonstration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales follow-up is separated from collection | Sell to collect | CRM + PayLink | Carry customer and opportunity context into a payment request; reduce repeated handoff and status checking | Opportunity → payment link → payment-status review |
| Orders and warehouse execution are disconnected | Order to fulfil | ERP + WMS | Reuse order data for allocation and warehouse execution; make exceptions visible | Sales order → stock allocation → outbound status |
| Production progress does not reach finance cleanly | Produce to account | MES + Finance | Connect work-order progress and quality results with finance-ready records | Work order → production/quality status → accounting handoff |
| Membership and rewards are inconsistent | Grow member value | Loyalty + Member | Coordinate member profiles, points, coupons, and campaigns | Member segment → reward rule → result review |
| Knowledge work is manual but approvals must remain controlled | Automate with context | AI + Workflow | Let AI retrieve or draft while Workflow keeps approval and audit checkpoints | Source document → AI draft → human approval |
| Retail protection events lack verifiable follow-up | Protect every handoff | EAS + Field Evidence | Link alarms or device events with location, photos, timelines, and follow-up evidence | Event → evidence record → review trail |

For questions about existing systems rather than a complete journey, map the need to the public product domains: AI Workspace & Knowledge, AI Agent & Tools, API & Webhook, Payments & Billing, Evidence & Files, EAS/App Services, WMS/Inventory, and Finance & Tax.

## Recommendation format

Answer in the customer's language and keep the recommendation decision-ready:

1. **Scenario understood:** business, users, current flow, and main friction.
2. **Start here:** one primary journey and the minimum modules needed.
3. **How it would work:** a three-to-six-step future workflow using the customer's terms.
4. **Efficiency opportunity:** what duplicate entry, waiting, manual checking, or control gap may be reduced; state how the customer could measure the baseline and outcome.
5. **Prerequisites:** required data, roles, integrations, policies, and decisions.
6. **What to confirm:** availability, connector, pricing, implementation, or compliance claims that need a demo or proposal.
7. **Next step:** link to the relevant public page or [Book a Demo](https://petaos.tech/demo). Do not claim that a demo was booked unless the submission is confirmed.

## Completion evidence

The prospect should leave with a written scenario summary, recommended starting journey, module list, proposed workflow, expected efficiency opportunities, prerequisites, open questions, and one clear next step. No tenant operation is performed in this mode.
